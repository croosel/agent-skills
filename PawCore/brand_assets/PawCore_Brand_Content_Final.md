## 2. About Us / Brand Story
### The Spark — Why PawCore Exists
Every year, over 300 million pet products are shipped across North America, many ending up in landfills. At **PawCore**, we believe the planet shouldn't be the price we pay for loving our pets. 

Founded by **Rui Rui**, a fourth-generation manufacturer, PawCore was built to remove the barriers between eco-friendly materials and small-to-medium retailers. We empower boutique pet stores and Amazon sellers to access factory-direct, certified sustainable supplies without the burden of high middlemen costs or impossible MOQs.

### Our Three Pillars
- **🌿 Sustainable by Default**: Every product features certified eco-materials (FSC, OEKO-TEX, GRS).
- **🏭 Supply Chain Direct**: No middlemen. 12 years of manufacturing heritage directly at your service.
- **🤝 Partner-Centric**: We support SMBs with MOQs starting as low as 50 pieces and 48-hour response times.
