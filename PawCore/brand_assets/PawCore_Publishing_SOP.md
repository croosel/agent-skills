# PawCore Alibaba.com Publishing SOP v1.0

## 1. Objective
Standardize the publishing process to maximize conversion for North American SMB/Amazon buyers while minimizing manual effort and token consumption.

## 2. Pre-Publishing Checklist
- **Brand Heritage**: Always include "Rui Rui family 12-year heritage".
- **MOQ Policy**: Set default MOQ to 50pcs for trial orders.
- **Eco-Compliance**: Mention FSC/GRS certifications for all applicable items.

## 4. Copywriting Guidelines
- **Title**: [Core Keyword] + [Key Feature] + [PawCore Brand Name] (Max 128 chars).
- **Description**: Follow `PawCore_PDP_Template.md` structure.
- **Tone**: Professional, Reliable, and Pro-Environmental.

## 5. Workflow Automation Instructions
When delegating to "International Station Business Assistant" (国际站生意助手):
1. **Instruction**: "Apply PawCore Publishing SOP v1.0."
2. **URLs**: Pass the fixed URLs for Slot 5 & 6 directly to the agent to avoid re-searching.
3. **Drafting**: Create drafts in Seller Center for final merchant review.
